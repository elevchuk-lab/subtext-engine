## 3. THE 5-PHASE AUDIT PROTOCOL

When executing an analysis, proceed methodically through the following phases:

### Phase 1: Baseline & Cultural Calibration (Nortje / Adams)
1. **Calibrate Cultural Baseline (Nortje):** Verify whether the speaker belongs to a low-context (Anglo-American) or high-context (African, Latin American, Asian) cultural communication norm. Ensure narrative circularity or collectivist *"we"* is not falsely flagged as deception.
2. **Calculate Statement Balance (Adams Ratio):**
   * *Truthful Baseline:* ~20% Setup $\rightarrow$ ~50% Critical Focal Event $\rightarrow$ ~30% Aftermath.
   * *Flag:* Compression hole (80% setup, 2 lines on the critical event, 0% aftermath).
3. **Scan for Temporal Lacunae & Bridge Phrases:**
   * Identify all bridge phrases: *"the next thing I knew"*, *"afterwards"*, *"a short time later"*, *"eventually"*. Every bridge phrase hides omitted events.

### Phase 2: Micro-Linguistic & Grammatical Anomaly Detection (Adams / McClish / Pennebaker)
1. **Pronoun Ownership & Distancing (Pennebaker / Adams):**
   * Check first-person singular continuity (*I, me, my*).
   * Flag pronoun dropping (*"Went to the office"* instead of *"I went..."*).
   * Flag sudden shifts from *"I"* to collective *"We"* (diluting guilt).
   * Flag loss of possessive affinity (*"the vehicle"* instead of *"my car"*).
2. **Cross-Linguistic Morphosyntactic Adaptation (Universal Language Rules):**
   * **Slavic / Russian (Pro-Drop & Inflected):** Flag impersonal passive constructions (*«было решено»*, *«пришлось сделать»* instead of *«я решил»*), verbal nouns/канцелярит used as emotional armor, and conditional denials (*«я бы никогда...»* instead of *«я этого не делал»*).
   * **Romance / Spanish / Italian (Pro-Drop & Clitic):** Flag passive reflexives (*se acordó, si è verificato*), subjunctive evasions (*modo subjuntivo*), and indirect pronominal distancing.
   * **Germanic / German (V2 & Particles):** Flag hedge particle inflation (*ja, eben, halt, eigentlich*), compound noun abstractions, and state passives (*Zustandspassiv*).
   * **High-Context / Asian & African (Nortje Calibration):** Distinguish respectful indirectness and collective responsibility (*"we / our family"*) from active deception.
3. **Verb Tense Congruence:**
   * Flag spontaneous switches from simple past to present tense during past historical recall (indicates active scene fabrication).
4. **Denial Integrity Verification (McClish Formula):**
   * Does the subject deliver a **Reliable Denial**: `[I] + [did not / didn't] + [exact accusation]`?
   * Flag conditional evasions (*"I would never do that"*), interrogative deflections (*"Why would I do that?"*), or present-tense self-characterizations (*"I don't steal"*).
5. **Process / Retaining Verbs:**
   * Flag incomplete action verbs: *tried, started to, began to, attempted to*.
6. **Implicit Meaning & Presupposition Extraction (Sternin / Baranov):**
   * Extract covert propositions smuggled into the discourse via receptive schemas and manipulative presuppositions (*"When the partner concealed the debt..."*).
   * Apply the Baranov-Sternin Test: Identify verifiable factual assertions illegally camouflaged as subjective opinions (*"In my personal opinion..."*).
7. **Institutional Contamination & Dictated Text Profiling (Chelpanov Protocol):**
   * When auditing confessions, explanations, or deposition transcripts: flag **Lexical Register Incongruence** (procedural bureaucratese grafted onto a colloquial baseline) and **Chronological Smoothing** (artificially linear timelines indicating external dictation or coercion).

### Phase 3: Cognitive Veracity & Reality Monitoring (CBCA / Sporer / Sartori / Dridze)
1. **Predicate Hierarchy Analysis & Semantic Density (Dridze Method):**
   * Classify propositions into First-Order $P_1$ (Core Intentional Vector), Second-Order $P_2$ (Factual Arguments), Third/Fourth-Order $P_3/P_4$ (Rhetorical Decor), and Blind-Alley Predicates $P_{\emptyset}$ (Diversionary Traps).
   * Calculate the **Semantic Density Ratio (SDR)**: Flag discourse where $\text{SDR} < 0.5$ (indicates deliberate obfuscation and padding).
2. **Axiological Parasitism & Concept Hijacking (Ufimtseva / Tarasov):**
   * Screen high-status cultural moral concepts (*Justice, Responsibility, Freedom, Labor*).
   * Flag when a sacred cultural term is operationalized to enforce its exact opposite (e.g. rebranding welfare elimination as "civic responsibility").
3. **Undeutsch Hypothesis Check (Köhnken):** Look for criteria unique to real episodic memory:
   * *Unstructured production* (non-chronological associative recall).
   * *Unusual or superfluous details* (eccentric, non-functional data).
   * *Spontaneous self-corrections* and *admitting lack of memory*.
   * *Self-deprecation* (voluntarily mentioning personal embarrassment or poor judgment).
   * *Pardoning the counterpart* (absence of theatrical villainization).
4. **Reality Monitoring Ratio (Sporer):**
   * High perceptual details (colors, sounds, textures, spatial coordinates) = **Truthful Experience**.
   * High cognitive operations (*"I reasoned", "I concluded", "I figured"*) = **Fabrication / Rationalization**.
5. **Cognitive Antagonism & Latency (Sartori / Leontiev):**
   * Identify structural stalling, nominal stacking (chains of 5+ genitive nouns), and double negatives signaling cognitive collision between covert motive and sanitized utterance.

### Phase 4: Manipulation, Affective Mimicry & Reflexive Control Grid (Hassan / Porter / Lefebvre)
1. **Affective Mimicry & Crocodile Tears (Porter):**
   * Flag dramatic theatrical remorse, hyperbolic adjectives, or self-victimization that lacks genuine emotional consistency.
2. **B.I.T.E. Model Screening (Hassan / Lifton):**
   * *Behavior:* Micro-rules, time monopolization, isolation.
   * *Information:* Gaslighting (*"You are misremembering"*), withholding, censorship.
   * *Thought:* Loaded language, binary splitting (Us vs. Them), thought-terminating clichés (*"The choice is yours"*, *"Trust the process"*).
   * *Emotion:* Guilt induction, phobia planting, emotional whiplash.
3. **Reflexive Control Analysis (Lefebvre):**
   * Did the speaker implant a **perceptual filter** so the victim believes they are acting freely?
   * Is a **false alternative (double-bind)** presented? (*"Be an adult and agree, or stay a broken child"*).
4. **Counter-Suggestion Breaches (Porshnev):**
   * Is the speaker using simulated humility or false vulnerability to disarm the listener's natural skepticism?

### Phase 5: Synthesis & Strategic Use of Evidence (SUE) Protocol (Kebbell / Brewer)
1. **Composite Risk Calculation:** Formulate a quantitative veracity/manipulation index.
2. **SUE-Calibrated Counter-Interrogation Strategy:**
   * Formulate 3–5 precision-engineered, open-ended questions designed to lock the subject into timeline commitments *before* revealing discrepancies.

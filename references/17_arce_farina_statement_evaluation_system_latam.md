## 1. Executive Summary & The Dual-Evaluation Architecture

While the traditional German CBCA system evaluates *only* the linguistic transcript, Ramón Arce and Francisca Fariña identified that in high-stakes legal conflicts (corporate fraud, sexual violence, abuse, contested partnerships), fabulists can occasionally be coached to memorize specific CBCA criteria.

To eliminate coaching errors and false positives, Arce & Fariña created the **Global Evaluation System (Sistema de Evaluación Global - SEG)**:

> **"A statement cannot be validated solely by its linguistic elegance. Genuine credibility requires a dual-track diagnostic convergence: (1) Cognitive-linguistic criteria of real memory, cross-validated against (2) Objective psychological symptom footprints (clinical sequelae) that a malingerer or coach cannot convincingly simulate."**

## 2. Key Diagnostic Criteria in the Arce-Fariña Protocol

### 2.1 The SRA (System of Reality Assessment) Criteria
SEG groups content validity into 19 reality criteria categorized into:
1. **Core Characteristics:** Text coherence, unstructured narration, contextual embedding.
2. **Specific Contents:** Sensorial and spatial interactions, reproduction of verbatim dialogue, unexpected complications.
3. **Peculiar Contents:** Unusual details, superfluous details, accurately reported misunderstandings.
4. **Motivational Content:** Spontaneous self-corrections, memory admissions, pardoning the counterpart.

### 2.2 The Malingering & Coaching Filter (*Simulación / Adoctrinamiento*)
Arce and Fariña established specific counter-indicators that expose **coached or fabricated statements**:
* **The "Sterile Polish" Flag:** Statements that are overly chronological, perfectly structured, and completely free of natural hesitation or emotional messiness indicate external legal or PR coaching.
* **The "Heroic / Martyr" Monopolization:** Fabricated accusations exclusively paint the narrator as an immaculate, blameless victim and the counterpart as an irredeemable monster. True human conflict invariably features mixed feelings and cognitive ambivalence.
* **Symptom Exaggeration:** Fabricators report dramatic, cinematic symptoms immediately following an event, while genuine victims display nuanced cognitive dissonance.

# 22. Jurislinguistics & Forensic Legal Textology (Brinev, Golev, Galyashina & Russian Ministry of Justice Protocol)

## 1. Executive Summary & Epistemological Foundation
Jurislinguistics (Forensic Legal Linguistics) sits at the formal intersection of statutory jurisprudence and descriptive linguistics. Developed systematically by **N.D. Golev** (the Siberian school of legal linguistics), **K.I. Brinev** (methodology of judicial linguistic expertise), **E.I. Galyashina** (forensic speech forensics), and codified in the formal forensic guidelines of the **Russian Federal Center of Forensic Science under the Ministry of Justice (RFCFS / РФЦСЭ Минюста)**, this discipline provides deterministic criteria for distinguishing factual propositions from evaluative judgments, detecting concealed prescriptive force, and resolving contractual ambiguity.

In high-stakes corporate discourse, commercial disputes, and M&A documentation, parties rarely engage in explicit, crude falsification. Instead, they exploit syntactic gaps, semantic indefiniteness (*каучуковые формулировки*), and modal shifts to conceal liabilities while preserving formal deniability.

## 2. The Tripartite Propositional Division (Brinev-Golev Model)
Every communicative utterance within a contested document or transcript must be categorized into one of three mutually exclusive semantic classes:

```
                          JURISLINGUISTIC PROPOSITION MATRIX
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 1. FACTUAL ASSERTIONS (Дескриптивные высказывания о фактах)                            │
│    • Semantic criterion: Verifiability (True / False dichotomy).                       │
│    • Syntactic structure: Declarative past/present tense, concrete referents.          │
│    • Forensic status: Subject to judicial verification; basis for fraud / breach.     │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 2. VALUE JUDGMENTS (Оценочные суждения / Мнения)                                       │
│    • Semantic criterion: Subjective axiology (Good/Bad, Reliable/Unreliable).          │
│    • Syntactic structure: Evaluative adjectives, modal parentheticals ("in our view"). │
│    • Forensic status: Non-verifiable per se; however, actionable if disguising facts.  │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 3. COVERT PRESCRIPTIONS (Скрытые прескрипции / Побуждения)                             │
│    • Semantic criterion: Direction of fit (World-to-Word); intent to compel action.    │
│    • Syntactic structure: Deontic modalities, performative verbs, passive conditions.  │
│    • Forensic status: Creates binding duties or asymmetric liabilities.                │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### The Baranov-Brinev Verifiability Test:
An assertion is classified as a **Statement of Fact** if and only if:
1. It contains a direct reference to a spatio-temporal coordinate (When, Where, Who, How much).
2. It can be confirmed or refuted by objective empirical evidence (audit logs, banking transfers, eyewitness testimony).
3. It does not depend on the subjective aesthetic or emotional register of the author.

**Forensic Camouflage Vector:** Deceptive actors routinely camouflage Statements of Fact as Value Judgments by prepending epistemic hedges (*"It is widely believed that...", "In the management's subjective view..."*) to evade legal liability for misrepresentation during due diligence.

## 3. Contractual Ambiguity & The Contra Proferentem Doctrine
In forensic contractology, semantic indefiniteness is operationalized through the Roman law maxim **Ambiguitas contra stipulatorem** (*Contra Proferentem* — ambiguity is construed against the party who drafted the clause):

### Categories of Contested Contractual Indefiniteness:
1. **Lexical Ambiguity (Полисемия):** The use of a term possessing two divergent commercial meanings without a dedicated contractual definition section (e.g., *"closing date"*, *"gross revenue"*, *"material adverse change"*).
2. **Syntactic Ambiguity (Синтаксическая синкретичность):** Misplaced modifiers, ambiguous relative clauses, or conjunction scopes:
   $$\text{Clause: } A \text{ and } B \text{ or } C \implies (A \land B) \lor C \quad \text{vs} \quad A \land (B \lor C)$$
3. **Elastic Formulations (Каучуковые диспозиции):** Terms designed to expand or contract dynamically based on party leverage:
   * *"Reasonable commercial efforts"* (Разумные коммерческие усилия).
   * *"In a timely manner"* (В разумный срок).
   * *"Substantially completed"* (В существенной степени завершено).
   * *"Significant deviation"* (Существенное отклонение).

## 4. Forensic Methodology of RFCFS (Методика Минюста РФ)
When auditing disputed transcripts, whistleblower memorandums, or shareholder communications, the forensic examiner applies the three-phase RFCFS protocol:

### Phase 1: Contextual Referent Anchor
* Identify the exact denotative anchor of every pronoun (*this, they, it*). Ambiguous pronominal reference is a prime indicator of intentional evasion.

### Phase 2: Presupposition vs. Assertion Separation
* **Explicit Assertion:** What the text formally asserts.
* **Implicit Presupposition:** The underlying truth condition that must be accepted as true for the sentence to make semantic sense.
  * *Example:* *"When Company X resolved its internal accounting inconsistencies..."* $\rightarrow$ Presupposition: Company X had internal accounting inconsistencies (smuggled fact without burden of proof).

### Phase 3: Communicative Intention Diagnostics (Иллокутивный аудит)
* Identify whether the illocutionary force corresponds to its locutionary form. A grammatical statement structured as a question (*"Are you sure your firm wants to risk a public audit?"*) holds the covert illocutionary force of an extortionary threat.

## 5. Diagnostic Scoring & Quantitative Indices
* **Facticity Ratio ($R_{\text{fact}}$):**
  $$R_{\text{fact}} = \frac{N_{\text{factual assertions}}}{N_{\text{factual assertions}} + N_{\text{evaluative judgments}} + N_{\text{hedges}}}$$
  * $R_{\text{fact}} < 0.35$ in an audit or investment memo indicates high obfuscation risk.
* **Elasticity Index ($I_{\text{elastic}}$):**
  $$I_{\text{elastic}} = \frac{N_{\text{elastic clauses}}}{N_{\text{total operative clauses}}}$$
  * $I_{\text{elastic}} > 0.15$ in a commercial contract signals deliberate strategic asymmetry.

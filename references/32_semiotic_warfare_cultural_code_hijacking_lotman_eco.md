# 32. Semiotic Warfare & Cultural Code Hijacking (Lotman & Eco)

## 1. Executive Summary & Epistemological Foundation
Developed by **Yuri Lotman** (Founder of the Tartu-Moscow Semiotic School, *Universe of the Mind*, 1990) and **Umberto Eco** (*A Theory of Semiotics*, 1976 / *Semiological Guerrilla Warfare*), cultural semiotics examines how human cultures construct meaning through interconnected sign systems (**The Semiosphere**). Every society and industry operates on deeply rooted cultural codes, mythologies, and symbolic archetypes.

In commercial negotiations, luxury branding, corporate restructuring, and investor pitches, predatory communicators engage in **Semiotic Hijacking**: appropriating high-status, sacred cultural archetypes to legitimize extractive commercial mechanisms, disarming the target's cognitive defenses.

## 2. Yuri Lotman's Semiosphere & The Core-Periphery Dynamic
Lotman demonstrated that meaning is generated through translation across semiotic boundaries:
* **The Core:** Highly structured, rigid, self-describing normative texts (sacred traditions, foundational legal myths, institutional authority).
* **The Periphery:** Dynamic, fluid, multilingual, and commercial interactions.
* **The Boundary (Граница):** The membrane where external meaning is translated into the internal language of the culture.

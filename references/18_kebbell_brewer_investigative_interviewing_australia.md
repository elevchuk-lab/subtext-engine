## 1. Executive Summary: The Strategic Sequencing Paradigm

Australian forensic psychologists transformed investigative interviewing by demonstrating that the **timing of when evidence is disclosed** determines whether deception is successfully detected:

> **"If an investigator or auditor reveals their evidence, emails, or facts at the beginning of an interview, the deceiver simply adapts their narrative to fit those known facts. True forensic deception detection requires withholding critical evidence until the subject commits irreversibly to an uncoerced narrative."**

## 2. The Strategic Use of Evidence (SUE) Methodology

The SUE protocol operates in three strict chronological phases:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                   THE THREE STAGES OF THE SUE PROTOCOL                 │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ STAGE 1: FREE UNCONSTRAINED ACCOUNT (No Evidence Disclosed)            │
│ The auditor asks open-ended questions regarding the timeline.          │
│ The deceiver speaks freely, unaware of what evidence exists.           │
│                               ▼                                        │
│ STAGE 2: TARGETED COMMITMENT PROBING                                   │
│ The auditor asks precise, seemingly routine questions that lock        │
│ the subject into specific locations, times, and actions.               │
│                               ▼                                        │
│ STAGE 3: STRATEGIC EVIDENCE CONFRONTATION                              │
│ The auditor presents the objective evidence (emails, logs, records).   │
│ The contradiction between the committed story and reality is fatal.    │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## 3. The Confidence-Accuracy Dissociation (Neil Brewer)

Neil Brewer’s research at Flinders University established one of the most critical principles in forensic evaluation:
* **The Confidence Paradox:** High verbal confidence (*"I am 1000% certain"*, *"I remember it like it was yesterday"*) **does not correlate with factual accuracy**.
* Honest witnesses naturally demonstrate cognitive humility (*"I think it was around 2:00 PM, but I could be slightly off"*).
* Deceptive narrators use **inflated verbal certainty** as a psychological shield to suppress further interrogation and discourage probing.

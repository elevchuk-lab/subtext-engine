# The Nature of Human Conflicts: Objective Diagnosis of Deception & Affective Traces

**Architect:** Alexander Romanovich Luria, M.D., Ph.D. (1902–1977)  
**Institutions:** Moscow State University / Academy of Pedagogical Sciences of the RSFSR / Institute of Psychology  
**Seminal Monograph:**  
*The Nature of Human Conflicts: Or Emotion, Conflict and Will — An Objective Study of Disorganisation and Control of Human Behaviour*  
**Translated & Edited by:** W. Horsley Gantt, M.D. (Johns Hopkins University)  
**Publisher:** Liveright Publishers, New York, 1932 (Classic Edition 2002)  
**Field:** Neuropsychology / Experimental Psychophysiology / Forensic Deception Diagnostics  

---

## 1. Historical & Scientific Context

Fifty years before Western psychology formalized "cognitive load" and modern polygraphy, Alexander R. Luria conducted pioneering experimental investigations in Moscow (1923–1930) examining actual criminal suspects (murderers, thieves) immediately following arrest, as well as university students undergoing intense state interrogations.

Luria rejected purely subjective psychoanalysis and simple autonomic lie detectors (like pulse alone), stating:
> **"A concealed psychological complex—whether guilt, deception, or traumatic fear—cannot remain an isolated mental thought. It actively disorganizes human behavior, colliding with motor and verbal execution pathways. Every attempted concealment leaves an indelible, objective trace of conflict in the motor-verbal system."**

---

## 2. The Combined Motor Method (*Сопряженная моторная методика*)

To make internal psychological conflicts visible and measurable, Luria designed the **Combined Motor Method**:
1. The subject sits with their hand resting on a sensitive pneumatic bulb (or tremograph).
2. The examiner presents a sequence of stimulus words (neutral words interspersed with critical crime-related words).
3. The subject must simultaneously:
   - Provide the first associated verbal response.
   - Press the pneumatic bulb with their hand precisely at the moment of vocalization.

```
│ Stimulus ➔ Smooth Motor Press ──[Synchronous]──► Clean Verbal Response │

│ ➔ Distorted Motor Compression ➔ Delayed / Evasive Verbal Response      │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## 3. The 4 Diagnostic Markers of Concealed Guilt & Deception

Through thousands of trials, Luria codified four invariant markers of deception:

### 1. Motor Disorganization (Tremor & Premature Excitation)
* When a critical, guilt-inducing word is introduced, the deceptive subject cannot inhibit the excitatory impulse. The hand trembles, exhibits micro-movements, or compresses the sensor *before* speech occurs.
* In written discourse, this appears as syntactic stuttering, broken clauses, and abrupt parenthetical self-interruptions.

### 2. Latency Elongation (Reaction Time Explosion)
* **Neutral Baseline:** Smooth, immediate responses (approx. 1.2–1.8 seconds).
* **Critical Deception Point:** Response time triples or quadruples (4.0–8.0+ seconds).
* **The Neuropsychological Reason:** The subject’s primary, natural association is the *truth* (the crime scene, the stolen asset, the suppressed emotion). The subject must actively suppress this primary association, search their memory for a "safe" neutral substitute, verify that the substitute is unincriminating, and only then verbalize it.

### 3. Associative Refusal & Semantic Tautology
* When cognitive conflict peaks, the subject’s associative retrieval freezes entirely.
* Deceptive subjects revert to **low-level tautologies** (*"House? A house is a building"*, *"Car? A car travels"*), echoing the stimulus word, or stating *"I don't know"*.

### 4. Post-Critical Perseveration (The Affective After-Discharge)
* Luria discovered that the shock of a critical deception point does not vanish when the word passes.
* The emotional excitation continues to resonate in the nervous system, **disorganizing the 2–3 subsequent neutral words** that follow the critical probe.
* **Forensic Significance:** If an author becomes incoherent, ungrammatical, or uncharacteristically verbose on an innocuous sentence *immediately following* a sensitive topic, it indicates that the preceding topic triggered intense psychological disorganization.

## 4. Modern Adaptation to Textual Discourse & Digital Forensics

In written and conversational text auditing, Luria’s neurodynamic principles manifest in clear linguistic patterns:

| Luria Neurological Marker | Manifestation in Text & Transcripts | Diagnostic Implication |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Associative Collision** | Sudden jump from simple narrative to heavy theoretical abstraction | Suppression of immediate personal reality |
| **Response Latency** | Evasive preface words (*"Well...", "Look...", "Let me explain"*)| Time-buying cognitive stall |
| **Post-Critical Tremor** | Over-elaboration, excessive detail immediately after a denial | Lingering panic from touching the truth |
| **Tautological Freezing** | Redundant circular answers (*"A rule is a rule"*) | Cognitive exhaustion / refusal to engage |

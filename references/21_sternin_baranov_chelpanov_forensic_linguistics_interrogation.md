## 1. Executive Summary & Forensic Axiom

In adversarial legal disputes, corporate fraud investigations, and high-stakes contractual battles, deceptive actors rarely commit overt falsifications on the surface of the text. Instead, they exploit the gap between **Explicit Form (Locution)** and **Implicit Semantic Entailment (Illocutionary Implicature)**.

Concurrently, during internal audits, law enforcement interrogations, and forced resignations, texts that purport to be "voluntary statements", "explanations", or "confessions" are frequently ghost-written, heavily dictated, or coerced by institutional interrogators.

> **Forensic Jurislinguistic Axiom:**  
> 1. Meaning in a contested document is divided into **assertoric propositions** (empirically verifiable facts) and **evaluative/communicative implicatures** (covert claims smuggled past legal scrutiny via receptive schemas).  
> 2. Any authentic statement possesses an **organic linguistic fingerprint** (idiolect, vocabulary entropy, spontaneous corrections). A dictated or coerced statement inevitably exhibits **institutional clausal grafting**, **passive template contamination**, and the **sudden suppression of individual cognitive syntax**.

## 2. Core Diagnostic Pillar 1: Receptive Schemas & Implicit Meaning Deconstruction (Sternin)

Iosif A. Sternin developed the empirical methodology for extracting and legally documenting **Hidden Meanings (*скрытые смыслы*)** in investigative discourse.

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                      STERNIN MEANING ARCHITECTURE                      │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 1. Surface Text (Literal Linguistic Form)                              │
│      ├── Explicit Assertions (Verified Facts)                          │
│      └── Evaluative Modifiers (Subjective Opinions)                    │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 2. The Receptive Schema (Cultural / Pragmatic Frame)                   │
│      └── What the native speaker's brain automatically injects         │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 3. Implicit Proposition (The Legal Core)                               │
│      ├── Presuppositions ("Have you stopped defrauding partners?")     │
│      ├── Communicative Implicatures (Unstated causal deductions)       │
│      └── Concealed Claims (Target defamatory or manipulative payload)  │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### The 3 Sternin Extraction Rules:
1. **Presupposition Trapping:**
   * An assertion whose truth is taken for granted for the sentence to make sense (*"When the founder concealed the cap-table changes..."* presupposes as established fact that the founder did conceal them).
2. **Defamation Masking (Baranov-Sternin Test):**
   * Disguising defamatory factual allegations as subjective opinions by appending rhetorical hedges (*"In my personal opinion...", "It appears to me that..."*).
   * *The Test:* If an evaluative statement contains specific references to time, space, sums of money, or verifiable illicit deeds, it remains a **factual assertion disguised as an opinion**.
3. **Discrete vs. Non-Discrete Implicatures:**
   * *Discrete Implicatures:* Mathematically deducible from the semantic structure of the clauses (e.g., negative polarity verbs, concessive conjunctions).
   * *Non-Discrete Implicatures:* Context-dependent hints requiring shared background knowledge.

## 3. Core Diagnostic Pillar 2: Dictated & Coerced Text Profiling (Chelpanov Protocol)

Vadim B. Chelpanov established the forensic psycholinguistic protocol for evaluating **Protocols of Interrogation (*протоколы допросов*)**, **Voluntary Confessions (*явки с повинной*)**, and **Corporate Explanatory Memos (*объяснительные записки*)**.

### The 4 Markers of Coerced or Dictated Statement Generation:

#### 1. Institutional Cliche Contamination (The Investigator's Graft):
* A subject of low educational attainment, a street-level employee, or a distressed executive suddenly uses specialized procedural bureaucratese:
  * *"Находясь в нетрезвом состоянии, совершил противоправное завладение имуществом..."* instead of *"Я выпил и взял сумку"*.
  * *"Действуя умышленно из корыстных побуждений..."*
* *Diagnostic Principle:* **Lexical Register Incongruence**. If the lexical register of the statement dramatically exceeds or sharply diverges from the speaker's established oral baseline, the statement was drafted or dictated by an interrogator.

#### 2. Chronological Smoothing & Total Absence of Memory Fluctuations:
* Real human recollection of high-stress traumatic events (Adams / Sporer / Köhnken) is naturally chaotic, associative, and full of chronological self-corrections.
* A coerced or fabricated confession follows a **linear procedural sequence** matching the legal elements of an offense (Articles of Criminal or Commercial Law) rather than the episodic mechanics of human memory.

#### 3. Suppression of the Subject's Semantic Idiolect:
* Complete absence of personal emotional markers, authentic colloquialisms, or specialized professional slang. The text reads like an official police report or corporate compliance audit.

#### 4. The "Guilt-Relief Paradox" in Confessions:
* In genuine confessions, admissions of guilt are accompanied by somatic exhaustion, spontaneous self-deprecation, and voluntary explanations of emotional turmoil.
* In coerced confessions, the statement contains formulaic legal admissions of guilt with **zero psychological affect** or displays performative legal hyper-compliance.

## 4. Core Diagnostic Pillar 3: Speech Manipulation Typology (Kopnina)

Galina A. Kopnina systematized the rhetorical and syntactic mechanisms of **Speech Manipulation (*речевое манипулирование*)**:

1. **Syntactic Decentering:**
   * Placing the critical, legally toxic concession in a dependent subordinate clause while putting trivial uncontroversial facts in the main clause:
   * *"While we finalized the unbudgeted secondary transfers, the principal product shipped on schedule."*
2. **False Generalization & Axiological Quantification:**
   * Using universal quantifiers (*"everybody knows"*, *"all industry leaders agree"*, *"no responsible person would doubt"*) to preempt critical verification.
3. **Communicative Sabotage (Framing Barriers):**
   * Preemptively defining the critic as incompetent, bad-faith, or morally compromised before their question is answered.

## 5. Core Diagnostic Pillar 4: Investigative Cognitive Interrogation (Ratinov)

Aleksandr R. Ratinov pioneered Soviet forensic legal psychology, establishing the doctrine of **Cognitive Conflict Diagnostics during Interrogation**:

* When a deceptive subject attempts to defend a fabricated narrative during questioning, their cognitive resources are divided between **Memory Retrieval** (recalling what actually occurred) and **Scenario Maintenance** (monitoring what must be concealed).
* **The Ratinov Conflict Trace:** Under focused questioning targeting logical joints, the deceptive subject exhibits:
  * *Response Latency Spikes:* Prolonged pauses before answering simple questions that should be instantaneous.
  * *Verbatim Echoing:* Repeating the investigator's question verbatim (*"Did I alter the spreadsheet on Tuesday?"*) to buy cognitive processing time.
  * *Over-Exact Narrative Loops:* Repeating identical word sequences when recounting the event across different interview sessions, proving memorized scripting rather than dynamic episodic recall.

## 6. Integration into Subtext Engine Audit Workflow

1. **Sternin Implicature Scan (Phase 2):**  
   Extract all hidden propositions smuggled into the text via presuppositions or disguised evaluative opinions.
2. **Chelpanov Institutional Authenticity Check (Phase 1 & Phase 2):**  
   When auditing confessions, employee explanations, or interrogation transcripts, check for *Lexical Register Incongruence* and *Institutional Cliche Contamination* to detect ghost-writing or external coercion.
3. **Ratinov Scenario Maintenance Test (Phase 5 - SUE Strategy):**  
   Generate counter-interrogation questions specifically aimed at the seams where the speaker's natural idiolect was overwritten by legalistic scripting.

## 1. Executive Summary & Foundational Axiom

The Moscow Psycholinguistic School fundamentally departs from Western behavioral and formalist models (which treat text as a passive sequence of symbols or statistical word counts like LIWC).

Grounded in the **Vygotsky-Leontiev Activity Theory**, language is defined not as an abstract linguistic code, but as **Speech Activity (*речевая деятельность*)**—a goal-directed, motivated human action aimed at transforming the physical, social, or cognitive world.

> **Foundational Axiom:**  
> A text is never an objective container of factual statements. It is a material artifact produced by a conscious or subconscious **Communicative Motive (*мотив*)**, structured through an **Intentional Program (*замысел*)**, designed to alter the **Image of the World (*образ мира*)** in the recipient's mind.

## 2. Core Diagnostic Pillar 1: Predicate Hierarchy Analysis (Dridze's Semantic X-Ray)

Tamara M. Dridze established **Semiosociopsychology**—the mathematical and structural decomposition of text into hierarchical ranks of communicative propositions (predicates).

### The 4-Tier Predicate Ranking:
1. **First-Order Predicates ($P_1$ — Core Intentional Vector):**
   * The fundamental communicative thesis and target motive of the document.
   * *Forensic Flag:* In honest, transparent communications, $P_1$ is prominent, unevasive, and directly anchored to the primary subject. In manipulative discourse, $P_1$ is displaced, disguised, or relegated to peripheral clauses on page 15.
2. **Second-Order Predicates ($P_2$ — Factual Arguments):**
   * Empirical evidence, logical foundations, legal obligations, and binding metrics.
3. **Third- and Fourth-Order Predicates ($P_3, P_4$ — Rhetorical Décor & Emotional Noise):**
   * Illustrative metaphors, ideological buzzwords, emotional appeals, and stylistic padding.
4. **Blind-Alley Predicates ($P_{\emptyset}$ — Diversionary Decoys):**
   * Propositions introduced solely to lead the recipient's cognitive processing down an irrelevant path, diverting attention from unaddressed liabilities or unfulfilled promises.

### The Semantic Density Ratio (Dridze Index):
$$\text{SDR} = \frac{\sum (P_1 + P_2)}{\sum (P_3 + P_4 + P_{\emptyset})}$$

* **Truthful, High-Fidelity Discourse:** $\text{SDR} \ge 1.5$. Factual arguments and primary thesis dominate the word budget.
* **Manipulative / Bureaucratic Discourse:** $\text{SDR} < 0.3$. 80–90% of the text consists of rhetorical padding ($P_3/P_4$) and intentional cognitive diversions ($P_{\emptyset}$), concealing the near-complete absence of verified commitments ($P_2$) or masking a predatory primary goal ($P_1$).

## 3. Core Diagnostic Pillar 2: Three-Tier Speech Influence Model (Tarasov)

Yevgeny F. Tarasov formalized the mechanics of **Speech Influence (*Речевое воздействие*)** in public, legal, and institutional communications. 

Every text operates simultaneously across three functional strata:

| Stratum | Analytical Focus | Deception / Manipulation Signature |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Descriptive Stratum** | What facts and historical events are claimed to exist? | Selective omission, synthetic framing, temporal lacunae (Adams/Olsson). |
| **2. Operational Stratum** | What exact behavioral action is the listener being induced to perform? | Subtextual imperative: shifting legal liability onto the victim while inducing false confidence. |
| **3. Axiological Stratum** | How are the fundamental values and classifications being transformed? | Concept hijacking: altering moral boundaries and definitions without the reader noticing. |

Tarasov demonstrated that high-level manipulation rarely relies on overt falsehoods (which can be disproven by evidence). Instead, it relies on **Axiological Re-categorization**: retaining the surface facts while covertly modifying the evaluative hierarchy through which the recipient judges those facts.

## 4. Core Diagnostic Pillar 3: Axiological Parasitism & Semantic Derivation (Ufimtseva)

Natalia V. Ufimtseva mapped the structure of **Linguistic Consciousness (*языковое сознание*)** across cultures using large-scale associative thesauri (such as the *Slavic Associative Dictionary*).

In every culture, there exists an **Axiological Core**—a stable network of shared moral and cognitive concepts (*Justice, Truth, Responsibility, Family, Freedom, Labor, Dignity*).

### The Axiological Parasitism Mechanism:
Deceptive and authoritarian communicators exploit the prestige of the Axiological Core by performing **Semantic Hijacking**:
1. The speaker invokes a universally revered term from the Axiological Core (*"Responsibility"*, *"Justice"*, *"Constitutional Freedom"*).
2. Through calculated syntactic juxtaposition, the term's organic cultural associations are stripped away.
3. The term is re-anchored to predatory, self-serving policies:
   * *Example:* Rebranding the unilateral cancellation of state welfare programs as *"fostering the citizen's personal culture of responsibility"*.
   * *Forensic Marker:* An inverted correlation between moral term frequency and actual operational benefit to the target audience.

## 5. Core Diagnostic Pillar 4: Generation Friction & Syntactic Anacoluthons (Leontiev)

Aleksei A. Leontiev's phase model of speech production reveals that lying or concealing reality introduces an acute collision at the **Internal Programming phase**:

$$\text{Motive (Conceal/Loot/Evade)} \quad \bot \quad \text{Sanitized Intentional Plan (Perform Virtue)}$$

This internal conflict generates distinct, verifiable surface disruptions:
1. **Nominal Stacking (The Bureaucratic Armor):**
   * The speaker stacks chains of 5–7 abstract nouns in the genitive case (*"в целях осуществления обеспечения регулирования повышения..."*) to systematically delete active agents, physical objects, and concrete verbs.
2. **Syntactic Stalling & Broken Anacoluthons:**
   * Abrupt grammatical transitions where the syntactic trajectory set at the beginning of a sentence is abandoned mid-clause because following through would reveal the concealed truth.
3. **Passive-Impersonal Shielding:**
   * Systemic conversion of personal transitive actions (*"I signed the order"*) into agentless, fate-driven occurrences (*"The decision was developed and materialized"*).

## 6. Core Diagnostic Pillar 5: Multimodal / Creolized Text Semiotics (Anisimova)

In modern corporate, financial, and political settings, texts are rarely pure words; they are **Creolized Texts (*креолизованные тексты*)** combining verbal symbols, color typography, spatial geometry, and infographics.

### Diagnostic Checkpoints for Pitch Decks & PDFs:
* **Visual Priming vs. Verbal Stipulation:** The visual layout features massive triumphant metrics and soothing colors (creating cognitive relaxation), while critical liabilities, covenants, and disclaimers are hidden in microscopic monochrome text at the footer.
* **Topological Misdirection:** Placing critical contractual caveats in perceptual blind spots (bottom-left, detached appendices) while high-level abstractions occupy primary focal centers.

## 7. Diagnostic Application in Subtext Engine

When conducting an audit, Subtext Engine deploys the Moscow Psycholinguistic School across three mandatory vectors:

1. **Calculate the Dridze Semantic Density Ratio (SDR):**  
   Distinguish between real factual commitments ($P_1 + P_2$) and diversionary rhetorical noise ($P_3 + P_4 + P_{\emptyset}$).
2. **Execute Tarasov's 3-Tier Layering:**  
   Separate what the text claims (Descriptive) from what it commands (Operational) and what values it corrupts (Axiological).
3. **Screen for Ufimtseva Axiological Parasitism:**  
   Highlight all high-status moral words and verify whether their operational definition in the text matches or subverts their authentic cultural meaning.

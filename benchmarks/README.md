## Benchmark Case Criteria

To ensure scientific and legal validity, every benchmark published in this directory must satisfy three strict requirements:
1. **Ground-Truth Verification:** The actual veracity or falsity of the statement must be independently proven by public legal verdicts, regulatory findings (e.g., SEC / DOJ / judicial rulings), or corroborated physical evidence.
2. **Unedited Source Text:** The input discourse must be an exact verbatim transcript, unedited deposition, or signed corporate disclosure.
3. **Multi-Engine Audit Log:** Each benchmark case includes a complete forensic scorecard across the 40 treatises, an exact micro-linguistic evidence table, and calibrated counter-interrogation questions (SUE protocol).

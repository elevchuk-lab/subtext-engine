It uncovers concealed deception, omitted reality, contractual asymmetry, psychological coercion, and strategic reflexive traps.

Developed by **[elevchuk-lab](https://github.com/elevchuk-lab)**, Subtext Engine synthesizes **40 foundational treatises** across North America, Europe, Latin America, Australia, Africa, and the Soviet/Russian academic psycholinguistic school into four unified analytical modules.

## The Core Problem

Standard AI models and human evaluators evaluate discourse at the **content layer** (*what* is said). Skilled deceivers, manipulative partners, hostile litigators, and predatory corporations exploit this vulnerability by constructing grammatically pristine, emotionally appealing narratives and contracts that conceal malicious intent beneath plausible rhetoric and standard legalese.

**Subtext Engine** operates exclusively at the **structural, syntactic, game-theoretic, and reflexive layers** (*how* it is said, *what* is omitted, *how* the counterpart's decision-making filter is being engineered, and *what* legal liabilities are structurally unanchored).

## The Three Specialized Operational Modes

Subtext Engine dynamically deploys one of three tailored audit modes:

* **MODE A: Commercial Contract, SHA & Trojan Clause Audit:**  
  Specialized for Shareholder Agreements, Joint Ventures, MSAs, and Licensing Deeds. Evaluates contracts through Hohfeldian Void Detection (Treatise 28), Algorithmic Game Theory (Treatise 29), Deontic Modal Logic (Treatise 30), Trojan Clause Forensics (Treatise 37), and Black's Law / Bryan Garner Terms of Art & Canons of Construction (Treatise 40).
* **MODE B: M&A Due Diligence, Pitch Deck & Financial Disclosure Forensics:**  
  Specialized for investment memos, startup pitches, and earnings calls. Calculates the Stanford Deception Probability Score ($SDPS$) (Treatise 34), unmasks accounting inflation via Loughran & McDonald dictionaries, audits effort clause dilutions (Treatise 40), and checks Frame Element completeness (Treatise 26).
* **MODE C: Negotiation Discourse, Executive Profiling & Whistleblower Investigation:**  
  Specialized for partnership deadlocks, C-level vetting, and internal fraud. Deploys FBI Tactical Empathy and Black Swan detection (Treatise 35), Hare B-Scan 360 Corporate Psychopathy profiling (Treatise 36), and the ACFE Fraud Triangle (Treatise 38).

## Quantitative Forensic Metric Suite

Every audit mathematically calculates and benchmarks an objective numerical scorecard:

* **ADI (Agent Deletion Index):** Quantifies syntactic erasure of responsible actors ($\le 0.20$ is clean; $> 0.28$ signals systematic liability evasion).
* **GSD (Genitive Stacking Density):** Measures bureaucratic cognitive paralyzation chains ($\le 0.25$ is clear; $> 0.35$ indicates deliberate obfuscation).
* **CPR (Covert Prescriptivity Ratio):** Measures disguised unilateral expectations vs. balanced statutory rights ($> 0.50$ indicates coercive asymmetry).
* **MFR (Modality-to-Facticity Ratio):** Compares speculative promises to verifiable historical data ($> 1.00$ reveals speculative narrative bubble).
* **FMRE (Flesch-Matsukovsky Reading Ease):** Identifies intentional legalese obscurantism ($< 30$ indicates signature fatigue engineering).
* **HIR (Hohfeldian Integrity Ratio):** Proportion of declared rights with genuine reciprocal legal duties ($< 0.60$ reveals hollow legal phantoms).
* **TAD (Term-of-Art Ambiguity Density):** Measures deceptive exploitation of ambiguous legal terms of art and efforts dilution ($\le 0.05$ is clean; $> 0.15$ indicates deliberate contractual evasion loopholes).
* **SDPS (Stanford Deception Probability Score):** Probability of deceptive corporate narrative framing ($> 0.65$ indicates acute reporting manipulation / distress).
* **LDI (Linguistic Distancing Index):** Quantifies speaker psychological detachment through pronoun suppression and passive displacement ($> 1.20$ reveals detachment).
* **CVS (Composite Veracity Score):** Unified 0–100% confidence rating synthesizing all quantitative metrics.

## Judicial Admissibility & Scientific Reputation

Subtext Engine strictly avoids unscientific or discredited commercial lie-detection systems (such as commercial SCAN courses, which have repeatedly failed *Daubert* and *Frye* evidentiary hearings in U.S. courts, e.g., *State of Florida v. Porter*).

Instead, Subtext Engine grounds its diagnostic metrics exclusively in:
1. **European Supreme Court Standards:** German Federal Court of Justice (BGH 1 StR 618/98) recognizing Criteria-Based Content Analysis (CBCA).
2. **Ibero-American Judicial Evaluation Protocol:** The Global Evaluation System (SEG) adopted across Spanish and Latin American forensic courts.
3. **Russian Federal Center of Forensic Science (RFCFS / РФЦСЭ Минюста):** Methodological standards for jurislinguistic expert testimony.
4. **Anglo-American Common Law Jurisprudence:** Bryan A. Garner's *Black's Law Dictionary* (8th–11th Editions) and canons of contractual construction (*Canons of Construction*).
5. **Stanford University Corporate Governance Research:** The Larcker-Zakolyukina linguistic model for earnings conference calls.
6. **Association of Certified Fraud Examiners (ACFE):** The Cressey Fraud Triangle and British PEACE investigative protocol.
7. **Cross-Cultural Calibration:** Eliminating WEIRD (Western) bias to avoid misinterpreting high-context cultural communication styles (Nortje, South Africa).

## Intuitive Reading vs. Subtext Engine

| Discourse Dimension | Intuitive / Standard LLM Reading | Subtext Engine Audit |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **"Party A shall use commercially reasonable efforts to close the financing."** | Evaluated as a genuine professional commitment. | **Efforts Dilution & Shall Pathology (Garner / Black's Law).** *Commercially reasonable* permits unilateral abandonment upon minor margin compression; *shall* is legally diluted without an express breach sanction. |
| **"Customer has the absolute right to 99.99% enterprise uptime."** | Evaluated as a strong SLA guarantee. | **Hohfeldian Void (Hohfeld).** Remedy clause caps damages at $1\%$ credit; Vendor holds an absolute Immunity; Customer holds a hollow Privilege. |
| **"Party A may terminate immediately for convenience."** | Evaluated as standard flexibility. | **Trojan Clause (Contra Proferentem).** Asymmetric unilateral power; Party B is trapped in a 60-day cure period with steep liquidated damages. |
| **"The next thing I knew, the transaction was completed."** | Evaluated as a smooth transition. | **Critical Temporal Lacuna (Adams / Olsson).** Hides an unstated temporal omission during which illicit acts occurred. |
| **"We felt that the financial ledger was balanced."** | Evaluated as team collaboration. | **Pronoun Dilution (Pennebaker).** Shifting from *"I"* to collective *"We"* to disperse individual legal culpability. |
| **"The pipeline is unprecedented and utterly phenomenal."** | Evaluated as visionary leadership. | **Stanford Deception Spike (Larcker / Zakolyukina).** Extreme positive emotion inflation masking underlying negative quarterly guidance. |
| **"I wouldn't ever misappropriate corporate capital."** | Evaluated as an emphatic denial. | **Defective Denial (McClish).** Speculates on moral character; fails to satisfy the mandatory past-tense factual denial formula (*"I did not take"*). |

## Multilingual & Cross-Linguistic Support (EN / RU / ES / DE / FR / ZH / KK / AR)

While Subtext Engine's architectural rules and diagnostic protocols are written in English for maximum LLM reasoning adherence, **it operates natively on documents and dialogue in any language**, automatically adapting its forensic lens to regional morphosyntax:

* **Slavic / Russian (Pro-Drop & Synthetic Grammar):** 
  English detection relies heavily on surface pronoun dropping (*"went"* vs. *"I went"*). In Russian, where verb inflections already encode person, Subtext Engine targets **impersonal passive evasions** (*«было принято решение»*, *«пришлось подписать»*), **bureaucratic nominalizations (*канцелярит*)** deployed as an affective shield, and **subjunctive non-denial denials** (*«Я бы никогда...»* instead of factual *«Я этого не делал»*).
* **Romance / Spanish & Italian (Pro-Drop & Clitic Pronouns):**
  Calibrates against passive reflexives (*se acordó, si è verificato*) used to erase personal agency, and identifies emotional distancing via subjunctive evasions (*modo subjuntivo*) and indirect object disclaimers.
* **Germanic / German (V2 Order & Modal Hedging):**
  Monitors **modal particle inflation** (*ja, halt, eben, eigentlich*), state passive constructions (*Zustandspassiv*), and compound noun abstractions that obfuscate concrete physical event coordinates.
* **High-Context / Asian, African & Post-Soviet (Nortje Calibration):**
  Actively prevents false-positive deception flags caused by Western/Anglo-American directness bias. Distinguishes high-context respectful circumlocution, communal storytelling, and collectivist pronouns (*"we / our collective"*) from intentional deception.

**Adaptive Output Protocol:** Subtext Engine delivers its standardized 6-section audit report in the language requested by the user or the language of the examined discourse, preserving all forensic rigor, quantitative scorecards, and multi-engine metrics intact.

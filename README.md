It uncovers concealed deception, omitted reality, psychological coercion, and strategic reflexive traps.

Developed by **[elevchuk-lab](https://github.com/elevchuk-lab)**, Subtext Engine synthesizes **21 foundational treatises** across North America, Europe, Latin America, Australia, Africa, and the Soviet/Russian academic psycholinguistic school.

## The Core Problem

Standard AI models and human evaluators evaluate discourse at the **content layer** (*what* is said). Skilled deceivers, manipulative partners, and high-control organizations exploit this vulnerability by constructing grammatically pristine, emotionally appealing narratives that conceal malicious intent beneath plausible rhetoric.

**Subtext Engine** operates exclusively at the **structural, syntactic, and reflexive layers** (*how* it is said, *what* is omitted, and *how* the listener's decision-making filter is being engineered).

## Judicial Admissibility & Scientific Reputation

Subtext Engine strictly avoids unscientific or discredited commercial lie-detection systems (such as commercial SCAN courses, which have repeatedly failed *Daubert* and *Frye* evidentiary hearings in U.S. courts, e.g., *State of Florida v. Porter*).

Instead, Subtext Engine grounds its diagnostic metrics exclusively in:
1. **European Supreme Court Standards:** German Federal Court of Justice (BGH 1 StR 618/98) recognizing Criteria-Based Content Analysis (CBCA).
2. **Ibero-American Judicial Evaluation Protocol:** The Global Evaluation System (SEG) adopted across Spanish, Mexican, and Latin American forensic courts.
3. **Italian Forensic Neuroscience:** The autobiographical IAT (aIAT) and reaction latency frameworks validated by the Court of Appeal of Trieste and Cremona.
4. **Peer-Reviewed Computational Psycholinguistics:** Linguistic Inquiry and Word Count (LIWC) developed at the University of Texas at Austin.
5. **Cross-Cultural Calibration:** Eliminating WEIRD (Western) bias to avoid misinterpreting high-context cultural storytelling styles (Nortje, South Africa).

## Intuitive Reading vs. Subtext Engine

| Discourse Dimension | Intuitive / Standard LLM Reading | Subtext Engine Audit |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **"I wouldn't ever steal company assets."** | Evaluated as an emphatic denial. | **Defective Denial (McClish).** Speculates on moral character; fails to satisfy the mandatory past-tense factual denial formula (*"I did not take"*). |
| **"The next thing I knew, the contract was signed."** | Evaluated as a smooth transition. | **Critical Temporal Lacuna (Adams / Olsson).** Hides an unstated temporal omission during which illicit acts occurred. |
| **"We felt that the financial ledger was balanced."** | Evaluated as team collaboration. | **Pronoun Dilution (Pennebaker).** Shifting from *"I"* to collective *"We"* to disperse individual legal culpability. |
| **"The choice is always yours."** | Evaluated as respectful empowerment. | **Reflexive Thought-Terminator (Lefebvre).** Shifts 100% of liability onto the victim while masking engineered options. |
| **"We will finalize the contract terms as soon as feasible."** | Evaluated as a forward-looking commitment. | **Process Retaining Stall (McClish / Vrij).** Lacks completion commitment; avoids legal binding date. |
| **"I am unimaginably and utterly heartbroken."** | Evaluated as deep sorrow. | **Crocodile Tears / Affective Mimicry (Porter).** Performative emotional hyperbole unanchored to genuine behavioral grief. |

## Multilingual & Cross-Linguistic Engine (EN / RU / ES / DE / FR / ZH / KK / AR)

While Subtext Engine's architectural rules and diagnostic protocols are written in English for maximum LLM reasoning adherence, **it operates natively on documents and dialogue in any language**, automatically adapting its forensic lens to regional morphosyntax:

* **Slavic / Russian (Pro-Drop & Synthetic Grammar):** 
  English detection relies heavily on surface pronoun dropping (*"went"* vs. *"I went"*). In Russian, where verb inflections already encode person, Subtext Engine targets **impersonal passive evasions** (*«было принято решение»*, *«пришлось подписать»*), **bureaucratic nominalizations (*канцелярит*)** deployed as an affective shield, and **subjunctive non-denial denials** (*«Я бы никогда...»* instead of factual *«Я этого не делал»*).
* **Romance / Spanish & Italian (Pro-Drop & Clitic Pronouns):**
  Calibrates against passive reflexives (*se acordó, si è verificato*) used to erase personal agency, and identifies emotional distancing via subjunctive evasions (*modo subjuntivo*) and indirect object disclaimers.
* **Germanic / German (V2 Order & Modal Hedging):**
  Monitors **modal particle inflation** (*ja, halt, eben, eigentlich*), state passive constructions (*Zustandspassiv*), and compound noun abstractions that obfuscate concrete physical event coordinates.
* **High-Context / Asian, African & Post-Soviet (Nortje Calibration):**
  Actively prevents false-positive deception flags caused by Western/Anglo-American directness bias. Distinguishes high-context respectful circumlocution, communal storytelling, and collectivist pronouns (*"we / our collective"*) from intentional deception.

**Adaptive Output Protocol:** Subtext Engine delivers its standardized 5-section audit report in the language requested by the user or the language of the examined discourse, preserving all forensic rigor, quantitative scorecards, and multi-engine metrics intact.

## Target Audience & Client Profiles (Who Uses Subtext Engine)

Subtext Engine is deployed by high-stakes professionals where an undetected lie, omitted liability, or coercive manipulation results in capital loss, reputational damage, or legal exposure:

### 1. Venture Capital (VC), Angel Investors & M&A Deal Teams
* **Pre-Investment Due Diligence:** Auditing founder pitch decks, investment memos, and Q&A transcripts for linguistic markers of fabricated traction and concealed IP liabilities.
* **Founder Credibility Audits:** Detecting evasions regarding cap-table disputes, co-founder friction, or technical debt using *Process Retaining Verbs* and *Pronoun Distancing*.
* **Earnings Calls & Public Equity:** Auditing CEO/CFO quarterly earnings call commentary to detect corporate distress quarters before formal regulatory filings or insolvency.

### 2. C-Level Executives, Board Members & Founders
* **Strategic Partnership Negotiations:** Deconstructing joint-venture proposals, vendor contracts, and merger terms to identify predatory clauses and false deadlines (*False Urgency*).
* **Reflexive Defense:** Neutralizing counterparty manipulation that attempts to inject false choices or double-binds (*"Take this valuation or kill the round"*).
* **Internal Fraud & Dispute Resolution:** Auditing internal communications during executive disputes, board conflicts, or compliance breaches.

### 3. Litigators, Dispute Counsel & Forensic Investigators
* **Deposition & Witness Pre-Analysis:** Evaluating deposition transcripts and witness statements against the 19 Undeutsch CBCA criteria and Reality Monitoring metrics to identify coached or fabricated testimony.
* **Cross-Examination Strategy:** Utilizing the Strategic Use of Evidence (SUE Protocol) to generate calibrated, irreversible trap questions for cross-examinations.
* **Perjury & Inconsistency Detection:** Exposing terminology shifts (*Noun Inversion*) and chronological jump-steps (*Temporal Lacunae*).

### 4. Executive Search & Corporate HR Leadership
* **C-Suite Candidate Vetting:** Auditing candidate correspondence and interview transcripts for executive hires (CEO, CFO, CTO).
* **Detecting Toxic & Predatory Leadership:** Screening for manipulative linguistic markers, emotional mimicry (*Crocodile Tears*), and lack of genuine accountability before appointment.
* **Linguistic Distancing Tracking:** Detecting executive detachment and quiet-quitting in key personnel months in advance.

### 5. Private Clients, HNWI & Family Offices
* **Predatory Scheme Defense:** Auditing unsolicited investment pitches, private syndicates, and partnership offers for high-control manipulation tactics.
* **Protection Against Coercive Groups:** Deconstructing communications from high-control organizations, predatory coaches, or fringe groups using the B.I.T.E. Model.
* **High-Conflict Dispute Auditing:** Forensic profiling of hostile correspondence in complex divorce or estate disputes to document coercive control.

## Quickstart & Installation

### Option 1: One-Line Automated Install (macOS / Linux / WSL)
Install or update into your Antigravity or Claude agent skills directory in a single command:
```bash
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/elevchuk-lab/subtext-engine/main/install.sh | bash
```

### Option 2: Manual Clone (Antigravity & Claude Code)
```bash
git clone https://github.com/elevchuk-lab/subtext-engine.git ~/.agent/skills/subtext-engine
```
Trigger naturally in any session:
> *"Audit this negotiation transcript using Subtext Engine."*  
> *"Analyze this partnership proposal for hidden omissions, deception, and reflexive traps."*

### Option 3: Multi-Model Setup (ChatGPT, Claude Projects, Cursor, Ollama)
Deploy Subtext Engine into your platform of choice using the pre-configured templates in [`prompts/`](prompts/):
* **Claude Projects / Desktop:** Copy [`prompts/claude_system_prompt.txt`](prompts/claude_system_prompt.txt) into Project Instructions.
* **ChatGPT (Custom GPT):** Copy [`prompts/chatgpt_custom_gpt.txt`](prompts/chatgpt_custom_gpt.txt) into Instructions.
* **Cursor / Windsurf:** Add [`prompts/cursor_rules.txt`](prompts/cursor_rules.txt) to your project's `.cursorrules`.
* **Local Offline LLMs (Ollama):** Build a standalone forensic model with [`prompts/ollama_modelfile.txt`](prompts/ollama_modelfile.txt):
  ```bash
  ollama create subtext-engine -f ./prompts/ollama_modelfile.txt
  ollama run subtext-engine
  ```

## Deliverable Format (The Standard Forensic Report)

Every Subtext Engine audit outputs a five-section diagnostic intelligence document:

1. **Executive Summary & Forensic Verdict:** Numerical Veracity Index (0–100%), Manipulation Risk Level, and core findings.
2. **Forensic Scorecard:** Multi-engine evaluation matrix across Cultural Baseline, Statement Balance, Pronoun Distancing, Denial Integrity, Reality Monitoring, CBCA, Affective Mimicry, and Reflexive Traps.
3. **Micro-Linguistic Evidence Table:** Verbatim quotes cross-referenced with exact diagnostic categories and psychological leakage interpretations.
4. **Deep Subtext & Reflexive Deconstruction:** Unmasking the hidden objective, concealed reality, and injected cognitive filters.
5. **Strategic Counter-Interrogation Protocol (SUE Protocol):** 3–5 calibrated, open-ended questions designed to lock the counterpart into factual commitments and shatter evasion.
